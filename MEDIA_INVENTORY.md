## Gaps

- **No wide landscape shots** among the roughly 70 posts reviewed. Almost every image is a portrait of a guest, in 4:5 or 1:1. A continuous camera journey cannot be built honestly from these, so the scenes should use deliberate cuts and sideways crops.
- **No camp, dinner, lantern or night-sky photos** in the posts reviewed. The Fire Shows, Thanoura and Belly Dance highlights are the likely source, and they need originals from the owner.
- **Wide originals** from the photoshoots, if they exist, would serve desktop far better than Instagram's cropped versions.
