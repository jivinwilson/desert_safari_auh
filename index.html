A typical evening

From last light to first star.

  1. 15:00

    Pick-up

    Your driver meets you at your Abu Dhabi hotel or home in an air-conditioned 4x4.

  2. 16:15

    Into the dunes

    Tyres are let down for grip, then the drive begins: steep crests, long slides and a few held breaths.

  3. 17:45

    Sunset stop

    Out of the car and onto the ridge. The sand turns from gold to copper to rose in about ten minutes.

18:30

Camp arrival.

Arabic coffee, dates, camel rides, sandboarding, henna painting and falcon photos while the fires are lit.

19:30

Dinner under lanterns.

Grilled meats, fresh salads and vegetarian dishes served at low tables on the sand.

20:30

Tanoura & stars.

Live performance by the fire, then quiet.

Look up.

21:30

Return.

Drop-off at your door, sand still in your shoes.

Times are indicative and shift with the season's sunset. Your confirmation will list exact timings.
